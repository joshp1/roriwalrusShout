Community

Rules

Version 2026-08-21

  1. Respect people.Disagree with ideas without attacking, threatening, or targeting members.
  2. Keep discussions readable.Use relevant topics, descriptive titles, and avoid repetitive or disruptive posting.
  3. Protect privacy.Do not publish private messages, personal information, or another person's media without permission.
  4. No spam or manipulation.Do not automate engagement, impersonate members, evade restrictions, or promote deceptive links.
  5. Follow moderator direction.Use the appropriate private channel to question a decision instead of derailing a discussion.
